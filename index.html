About Us

Artistry, made personal

Meet Deepi Jassal – Professional Makeup Artist. With over 10 years of experience in the beauty and makeup industry, Deepi Jassal is a passionate professional makeup artist, trainer and beauty expert who believes that every face tells a unique story.

Her diverse experience spans bridal and party makeup, backstage fashion shows, and e-commerce and professional photoshoots. Having also worked as a makeup trainer, she brings not only artistic creativity but also in-depth industry knowledge and technical expertise to every look she creates.

Deepi's passion lies in enhancing natural beauty while bringing each client's vision to life. Whether it's an elegant bridal look, glamorous party makeup or a professional photoshoot, she believes in creating personalised looks that reflect individuality, confidence and style.

At Makeup Studio by Deepi Jassal, beauty is more than makeup – it's artistry, made personal.

From intimate celebrations to grand weddings, the studio is committed to personalised beauty services: a consultation that listens first, products chosen for your skin, and a finish that lasts all day.
